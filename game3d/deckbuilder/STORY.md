# SPIRE OF KARRIDGE — the story (2026-08-06 rewrite)

**Era:** the Kingdom of Ankunyx, twenty years after Book 4. The Dragon Emperor's
peace holds; vampires are citizens, orcs are licensed, necromancy is legal only
under license — and nobody counts the gifted who go missing from the safe roads.

**This game is the Karridge arm of the Ankuspawn Conspiracy** (docs/LORE_BIBLE.md).
The Cult of Anku profiles the gifted, prices them in grey ledgers, and ships them
west at the new moon. Its founder is known only as THE MATRON. Per canon guardrails:
the conspiracy is never exposed, no named book character dies or learns of it, and
the game's victory is local — one pipeline burned, one shipment freed, the web intact.

**VESSIA** (original to this game): dark-elf warlock, Ashenveil-schooled and thrown
out of the academy for asking what the lower levels were for. Unlicensed, unimpressed,
fighting pit bouts for coin. She recognizes harvest-work when she smells it — because
she was taught by the people who invented it.

**THE LEDGER (the plot's spine, defined in her first scene):** the cult's catalog of
gifted people — each page a name, the gifts observed, and the asking price a buyer
will pay. Scouts write it; the new-moon wagons collect by it. Every act reframes it:
Act I, the champion was a page in it (and Vessia learns she's being written in);
Act II, it must travel to its buyer — so it can be followed; Act III, the Champ read
his own page and liked the number. The epilogue: the dancer's name is already in one,
and so is Vessia's.

## Act I — THE PIT OF KARRIDGE
The Pit's previous champion vanished the night after his biggest win — Bellow says
he ran, but his winnings still sit in Marlow's strongbox. Vessia reads it in one
line: *"Men who run, run WITH their money."* The Hound Master isn't just the pit's
beast-keeper — he watches the yard for somebody, counts what fighters can do, and
sells the counting. She beats it out of him: there's a list, and it was never his.
Marlow's warning closes the act: the quiet man at his bar was asking after HER.

## Act II — KARRIDGE, WEST WALL
Marlow's back room (five silver) gives the pattern: three vanishings, all gifted,
all at the new moon, wagons always west. The alley pipeline: the Hook, the Road
Gunner, the Stitcher (who *prepares* the merchandise), Gravehand. The Veiled Woman
shows where the harvest goes: a fertility elixir that cost her wedding ring —
brewed, though she won't ask, from somebody's daughter. The act boss is a LICENSED
Court Necromancer moonlighting for the cult: *"You have been appraised. The Matron
pays handsomely for your kind."* Act outro: ANKUNYX himself passes through Karridge,
untouchable — and Vessia, holding half a truth, lets him go. *"Patience is also a
weapon."*

## Act III — THE WEST ROAD, NEW MOON
The night shipment: a waystation whose freight is people. The caged Quarry Boy was
profiled at a fair — *"my name had a NUMBER next to it."* The crew: the Chain, the
Pyre, the Wall. And the boss twist: **THE CHAMP is the vanished champion.** Nobody
took him — he read his number in the grey book and SIGNED, guarding the road for
pay in thralls. His dying line: *"Tell Bellow... they were never going to remember
me anyway."* The wagon burns; the freed walk home.

## Epilogue — VARENHOLM
A playbill: ONE NIGHT ONLY — THE FIREBIRD OF VARENHOLM. The dancer (Cookie — the
approved cameo, never named in-game beyond her stage name) floods a packed hall
with felt emotion: Ember-work, the very grade the wagons roll west for. She tosses
the grim woman in the crowd a flower and tells her to come back. Vessia knows the
ledger already holds the dancer's name — and, somewhere in the Ashenveil, a page
with her own. *"Let the hand that writes come to collect. I will teach it a better
trade."*

# THE SECOND ROAD — TSUBAKI, THE MATRON'S BLADE (2026-08-08)

The other playthrough walks the villain's side of the same conspiracy. **TSUBAKI**
of the Ieyasu school (from Hiro's samurai reference sheet — "Victory is my nature")
is Lady Nyx's best student: a collector of the gifted, and the operative long ago
assigned the cult's oldest errand — the seduction of the Emperor himself, whose
ember life-force the Matron drinks to strengthen herself (Books 3–4 context; the
vial in her epilogue is that errand's fruit).

**Her acts:** the pipeline Vessia burned must be rebuilt. Act I — the Pit, under
new eyes: she fights as cover, and finds the TEMPEST SCHOOL (an eastern mercenary
house hired by families of the taken to hunt the cult) already watching; boss: THE
LONGBOW. Act II — the west wall route, re-opened over the school's objection;
boss: THE IRON PALM, who carries the names of the sold. Act III — the Tempest
House on the west road, burned politely; boss: THE STORM SAGE. Her cage stop frees
a cult courier who whispers of a mole; her tavern stop watches the Firebird and
files her. Epilogue — THE ASHENVEIL: the delivery, the vial, and the next
assignment: a man on the western roads, green eyes, no escort. The web has a new
spider. Guardrails hold: the school survives her (broken, not erased), the
conspiracy stays secret, and the Emperor never knows.

**Her deck (three schools, per Hiro):** BLEED (First Cut, Artery Cut — wounds tick
through block), CONDITIONAL FATAL STROKES (Sneak Opening: 1st turn only; Odd-Hour
Stroke: odd turns; Open the Red: bleeding foe; ICHIGEKI: odd turn + bleeding foe,
30 damage), and PARRY/COUNTER (Patient Defense and Perfect Parry bank FOCUS —
bonus action points next turn; Ieyasu Counter arms a RIPOSTE that answers any
guarded attack). Epic: TSUBAKI BLOOM — consume all Bleed for +4 damage a stack.
Every card carries its own generated animation.
