# Adventurer — bonus dialogue review

The current five bonus statements and twenty bonus responses for each of the 60 ordinary personalities: 300 statements and 1,200 response texts. Targeted duplicate wording has been revised; voice IDs are retained.

Historical cost of the original bonus batch: **1,500 recordings**, **44,862 credits**. This is not the cost or account balance of the later duplicate-line revision.

Replies stay matched to relationship and conversational purpose. Suitable lines are chosen randomly without reuse until the available set has been exhausted; the cycle continues across saves. This expands variety without changing how often a character is allowed to reply.

Four situational responses are shared between compatible neutral, friendly, and romantic banks. Those additional file paths reuse the same actor’s recording and do not require extra paid generations.
